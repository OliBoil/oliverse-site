Today was a different kind of day during my internship. Instead of spending the day in the workshop, I joined an on-site installation at an office in central Barcelona.

We installed a wall-mounted display for a meeting room: preparing the bracket, securing it to the wall, fitting the screen and making sure the setup was stable and properly aligned.

I also helped configure a Barco ClickShare system. The base unit was connected to the display via HDMI, while the ClickShare Button allows users to share content from their laptops wirelessly during meetings.

It was an interesting experience because it showed me how much planning, precision and teamwork go into a professional AV installation. It also reminded me that IT is not only software: physical infrastructure, cabling and reliable meeting-room technology matter just as much.

A valuable day of learning in a real working environment.
